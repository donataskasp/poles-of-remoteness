Poles of remoteness: eight redesigns

Each option is a working copy of the site in design/options/ on branch ui-options, running on the real published data for both regions and both languages. Every screenshot comes from a live browser with the real satellite imagery: Lithuania, the Scandinavian continent view, zoom 13 on pole 1, the About dialog, and the phone. Nothing is deployed and site/ is unchanged.

My recommendation: 02 Orienteering

It serves both audiences best. Map people read it instantly: poles are controls, the card is a control description sheet. Curious visitors still get a bold, legible site with the satellite imagery kept in full colour. It is the least gimmicky of the bold options and needs no product changes, only new styles, markup and one font.

  1. Boldest alternative: 01 Star atlas. The most memorable and the most beautiful on the continent view. The catch: it is dark only, and in low-remoteness countries like Lithuania, where most visitors land, the map is nearly empty.
  2. Calmest alternative: 07 Survey sheet. A precise map sheet with a live coordinate frame. It costs a little map area.

Tell me the number, or mix (for example "02 with 01's stars"). I then build it properly into site/: self-hosted fonts, the 256 KB budget, the tests, and the screenshot suite.

To click through an option locally, from the repo root: node dev/serve.mjs --site design/options/02-orienteering --data site/data --port 8302, then open http://localhost:8302/europe/lt. Each option's notes (fonts, changed files, risks, what shipping takes) are in design/briefs/<option>-notes.md, and the eight briefs are in design/briefs/DIRECTIONS.md.
