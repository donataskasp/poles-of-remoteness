# 04-park-brochure: "The Unigrid"

- **Thesis:** the site becomes a park brochure in the Unigrid grammar: a black title band, a strict grid, flat paper panels with black type and thin rules, and the map as the brochure's map panel.
- **First view (desktop):** a 96px black band whose big white line is the live headline ("Lithuania. 3.43 km from any drivable way."), with the site title and subtitle under it and region and language as white underlined text toggles on the right. Under it, the map takes the left two thirds and the right 468px column stacks two modules: the unit panel: name and rank, a 48px distance figure with "from anything drivable" beside it, the scenario switch, the hint and the islands toggle on one row, the actions, "Pole 1 of 10" with the ten chips on one row, and the facts in a tight two-column grid; the ranking module: its title is pinned and ruled, so rows scroll cleanly under a rule.
- **Signature:** the band headline follows the unit, the scenario, the islands toggle and the language. Second detail: the pole chips in the card are the same black squares with white numerals as the map markers, so the card and the map read as one key. The selected pole is brown and a size up in both places.
- **Type:** Public Sans 400/600/800 from Google Fonts (the US federal government face, Helvetica lineage). Latin plus latin-ext is roughly 55 to 60 KB of woff2. Labels come in three sizes only (13/15/22), plus two display sizes (the band line and the figure). All rules are horizontal or vertical, all corners square, no shadows.
- **Colour:** paper #fcfbf7, ink #121210, one accent (arrowhead brown #5e3a1c) used for the primary button, the selected chip and marker, links and focus. The remoteness bands use a park-map ramp from pale tan through sage to deep forest green (#ece3bd to #184427, alpha .74). The satellite is desaturated (`.basemap` filter) so the greens carry the reading.
- **Dark:** the band stays black. The paper turns charcoal #1c1c1a with off-white type and rules. The accent lightens to #dba36c for text, the button stays #8b5a2b, and the imagery is dimmed further.
- **Map furniture:** square black zoom buttons, a metric scale bar (new, via `L.control.scale`, desktop only), and a legend module with a visible title, a contiguous ramp that names "km" once, and the base map switch in its head. The readout is a black callout.
- **Phone:** the band carries the headline on two lines, with the title and toggles on a second row. The sheet face drops the name and distance, which the band already shows, and shows the active scenario letter in a black square, the rank line and a chevron. The unit panel's head is hidden inside the sheet.
- **About:** a spread with the black band as its title, the text in two ruled columns, and a sticky black close square.
- **Files changed:** `index.html` (band structure, legend module, ranking title, font link, SVG close icon) and `css/app.css` (a full rewrite). JS changed: `card.js`: band rendering, head and figure markup, the new summary face, facts as a two-column grid, SVG icons; `app.js`: passes the band element; the legend label drops "km" except on the last stop; `i18n.js`: `bandHeadline`, `bandWhatA`, `bandWhatB` and `figureFrom` in en and lt; `markers.js`: square icons, the active one bigger; `ranking.js`: the other scenario gets its own column; `map.js`: the scale control.
- **Product changes implied:** the full headline sentence moves from the card into the band, and the card shows it as name plus figure; the phone sheet face no longer repeats the name and distance; a new scale bar; the imagery is desaturated by default.
- **Risks:** The right column is still a shared budget. At 1440x900 in English the ranking shows 9 full rows. For Northwest Territories in Lithuanian (the name and the settlement both wrap) it shows about 3 rows before scrolling; the rest scroll under the ruled title.; The US-parks association may read oddly for Europe.; It is the most conservative of the bold options.; Desaturating the imagery costs some of the satellite's colour.
- **Detector:** 3 warnings of 2 kinds, both kept on purpose: `transition: height` on the phone sheet: the incumbent mechanism, driven by the measured `--sheet-h`.; About dialog "no inset": the band title bleeds to the dialog edge on purpose, and the text columns carry their own padding.
- **To ship:** self-host Public Sans (3 weights, latin plus latin-ext) inside the 256 KB first-screen budget; `dev/tests/card.test.mjs` asserts `card__headline` markup and the summary row's contents, so it needs rewriting for the band, head and figure split and the new summary face; ranking tests may check the `ranking__dist` markup; regenerate `docs/screenshots`.
