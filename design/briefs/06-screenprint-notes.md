# 06-screenprint: "Two passes"

- Thesis: readings A and B are two ink passes of one hand-pulled screenprint. A is printed in fluorescent pink, B in blue, and switching the scenario pulls the other pass.
- First view: a paper strip with the title in huge stencil caps, too big for the strip, so it runs over the top edge of the map. Below it the card is a printed ticket with registration crosses outside its corners and a perforated stub for the pole's facts. On the right the ranking is a printed list with big stencil rank numerals in the current ink, and the current unit's row is solid ink.
- Map: the satellite image is filtered to a pale grey print base (`.basemap` filter). The data pane is laid over it with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` (on black stock in dark: `screen` over a darkened photo). The six bands are one ink at rising density, and the step-wedge legend doubles as the print's control strip (moved to the bottom right of the map).
- Signature: the scenario switch. The whole page re-inks through `html[data-pass]`. The new map layer waits for its tiles, then slides in about 6px out of register and settles while the old ink fades (440 ms). The headline, the pressed switch, the rank numerals, the chips, the markers and the legend get the same slide. Titles carry a second copy in the other ink, 60%, offset 2px (3px on the big title). The selected marker gets the other ink overprinted, visibly offset.
- Fonts: Big Shoulders Stencil 700 to 900 (display letters). Digits come from its unbroken sibling Big Shoulders Display 700 to 900 through a `unicode-range` face named "Poster Figures", because the stencil "1" read as an "l". Also Archivo, variable wdth 75 to 100 and wght 400 to 700 (body, labels, tabular figures), both from Google Fonts with latin-ext. About 170 KB for the woff2 files in total.
- Colours: paper #f3eee2 with an inline SVG grain, black ink #1e1b18, pink #e5287a (text version #c2185f), blue #0b72b9 (#0a5f9e). Dark is black stock #171615 with light inks (#ff5fa2, #4fa6ee) and is fully designed.
- Files: index.html (fonts link, `data-pass`, `.print` titles, ranking title, SVG close button), css/app.css (rewritten). JS changed: app.js (one palette per scenario via probe elements, `pullPass` animation, `data-pass`, legend re-render on switch), card.js (headline markup, A/B ink buttons, authored SVG icons, flags dropped), ranking.js (flag column dropped), markers.js (28px), i18n.js (`applyDom` fills `data-print`). No new strings.
- Product changes implied: no flag emoji in the card, the summary or the ranking. The legend and basemap switch move to the bottom right on desktop. Every accent colour now follows the scenario.
- Risks: the stencil caps are loud against the dry voice. After review the three nearest bands were made denser in both schemes. The Lithuania patches now read at country zoom and the continent view keeps its gradient without going solid pink. Lithuania still looks sparse because most of its land is under 1 km from a road, which is left uncoloured by design. Multiply over forest turns the heavy bands toward maroon.
- Detector: 2 warnings left, both the phone sheet's `transition: height`, which the incumbent sheet mechanism uses (`--sheet-h`). Left as is.
- To ship: self-host the two fonts and subset them to latin and latin-ext to stay under the 256 KB first-screen budget. `dev/tests` that assert flag emoji in the card or ranking markup, the single-palette `createDetailOverlays` or `setScenario` removing the layer synchronously would need updating.
